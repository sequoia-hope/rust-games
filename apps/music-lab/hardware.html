Music Lab

The music engine of the rust-games (crates/music, Rust): electronic and orchestral tracks made from genre profiles and grammars with a seed, on emulated classic instruments (TB-303, Juno-style pads, Moog-style monos, DX-style FM, plucked and bowed strings, brass, TR-808, TR-909 and concert percussion) with a proper mix. It is the engine the games play.

Make a track

A seed and a genre make a whole track: one hook, stated sparse in the breakdown and in full at the drop; bass that picks up the next chord; 8-bar blocks with a turnaround. Déjà vu (after Mutable Instruments' Marbles) is how often a block plays the core pattern rather than one of its variations: high locks into a loop, low keeps moving. The style line names the sub-style, key and progression.

    Measure (offline, no ears)

    Renders 12 s from the fullest part of every song made this visit and compares loudness (RMS), peak, crest factor (peak over RMS: low is squashed, high is spiky), stereo width (side over mid) and the balance of the bands against the low band (40–150 Hz).
